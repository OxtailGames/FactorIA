Tidewater Market is a calm harbour town builder.

You start with a coast, a road and a handful of coins. Lay roads, put up houses, farms, a lumber camp and a quarry, and watch your villagers walk to work. Feed them, house them and keep them busy, and the town grows. Then build a pier, and the sea starts to answer.

BUILD A TOWN THAT WORKS
- 14 buildings: roads, houses, farms, fisher docks, lumber camps, quarries, sawmills, mills, bakeries, smokehouses, workshops, warehouses, markets and piers.
- Real people, real carriers: villagers walk to their jobs and goods are carried along your roads. A badly placed road costs you a day.
- Four seasons of ten days each. Grain, fish and bread have to be ready before winter.
- Desirability overlay: see at a glance where a house would be happy, in colour and with pips for colour-blind players.

TRADE WITH THE SEA
- Cogs, skiffs and carracks arrive with orders. Fill them for coins and reputation, miss them and they remember.
- 11 event cards bring storms, festivals and strangers with a proposal. Every card asks for a choice.

FOUR WAYS TO PLAY
- First Harbour teaches the basics.
- River Mills asks for flour and bread.
- Winter Trade tests your stores against the cold.
- Sandbox has no goals: build what you like.

MADE TO BE COMFORTABLE
- Three save slots with an autosave in each.
- Five volume sliders, reduced motion, hold-free controls, UI size, full key rebinding.
- Mouse, keyboard or gamepad (with a cursor).
- Seven achievements.

Tidewater Market collects nothing and needs no account.
