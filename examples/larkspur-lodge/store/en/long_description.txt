Snow has closed the pass. The telephone is dead, the storm has the power, and Dr. Vale's supper is still on the table. You are the night porter of Larkspur Lodge, you hold the keys, and until morning you are the one who can open every door.

Larkspur Lodge is a narrative mystery in a small 3D world. Walk the lodge by night (hall, dining room, kitchen, library, guest rooms, the cellar), talk to the people under its roof, read what they left behind and decide whom to believe.

Five chapters, three endings. What you ask, what you keep to yourself and who trusts you decide whether the night ends with the truth at dawn, a quiet burial of it, or a long winter.

- Talk with Mara the owner, Hedda the housekeeper, Tobias the cook, Captain Haas, the journalist Lucian and, in the end, Dr. Vale himself. Your answers are remembered.
- Search rooms, collect keys and clues, combine items and look at them in 3D.
- Keep a notebook that fills itself in, and link clues on the deduction board (with undo) to reach conclusions the story acts on.
- Solve four puzzles: a desk drawer lock, a torn letter, a coach timetable and the cellar fuse box that ends the storm.
- Watch ten short cut-scenes, skippable at any time.
- Save in five slots plus an automatic save at every chapter; after the first ending you can start again from any chapter you reached.
- Play your way: keyboard and mouse, a gamepad, or touch. Click or tap to walk round furniture, or use WASD or the stick.
- Made to be read comfortably: text size, caption backgrounds and speaker names, text speed and auto-advance, reduced motion, colour filters, full key and button rebinding, and menus that can be read aloud.

No violence is shown, no online connection is needed and there are no purchases or ads.
