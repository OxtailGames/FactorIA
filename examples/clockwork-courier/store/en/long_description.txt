Wind up. Run. Deliver.

You are a little clockwork courier with one job: get the parcel to its door before your spring runs down. Every second on the dial counts, and the town is full of things that want to slow you down.

RUN, JUMP AND CLIMB
- Leap across rooftops, cross rope bridges, ride see-saws and bounce over crates.
- Walk softly or sprint: push the stick gently to walk, all the way to run.
- Wind keys along the way give you more time on the spring. Checkpoint flags keep a mistake from costing the whole run.

SIX HAND-MADE LEVELS IN TWO DISTRICTS
- Rooftops: three sunlit levels of chimneys, gaps and a clock square.
- Night Market: three lantern-lit levels of stalls, narrow lanes and sleepy watchdogs.
- Every level has a main route and hidden shortcuts for couriers who know the town.

GUARD DOGS
Some dogs stand guard, some patrol, and all of them have sharp eyes. Walk slowly to sneak past, lead them away, or outrun them: you are a little faster. A dog that gives up the chase counts; a dog that catches you sends you back to the checkpoint.

STARS, SHORTCUTS AND A DAILY ROUTE
- Earn up to three stars per level: deliver fast, deliver with spring to spare, deliver the extra parcel.
- 18 stars in all, 14 achievements, best-time boards and a new daily route every day.
- Paint your robot copper or neon green. Colours are cosmetic and never change how the game plays.

MADE FOR EVERYONE
- Play with touch, a gamepad or the keyboard. On a phone the on-screen stick and buttons appear when you touch the screen.
- Tap-to-move, adjustable camera, text size, captions for sounds, vibration on or off.
- Everything that matters is readable without colour: hazards differ by shape, not only by hue.
- English and Spanish.

FAIR AND CALM
- Play the whole game for free. There is an optional purchase to remove the offer of a short ad that gives extra time on a level you have just failed. Nothing is ever required to buy.
- No account needed, no tracking and no ads while you play.

Clockwork Courier is made with the FactorIA engine by Oxtail Games.
